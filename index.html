Jazmin Ricks, MPH

Public health researcher and program strategist

Jazmin brings nine years of experience in program evaluation, grant development, research design, and data analysis, grounded in public health and equity. She has helped secure more than $1.1 million in competitive awards, and has designed and led multi-partner initiatives on water equity, flood resilience, illegal dumping, and access to green space, using an approach rooted in shared governance and in directing resources to community partners.

Her expertise spans mixed-methods design, survey and focus group research, multi-criteria decision analysis, spatial analysis and dashboards, grant development, and program evaluation. She has led multi-stakeholder projects involving reviews of existing plans, primary and administrative data collection, community advisory structures, and collaborative synthesis to set planning and funding priorities. She holds a Master of Public Health in Environmental Health from the University of Pennsylvania.
